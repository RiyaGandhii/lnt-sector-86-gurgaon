Frequently Asked Questions

What is L&T Sector 86 Gurgaon?

L&T Sector 86, Gurgaon (marketed as L&T Aravista) is a luxury residential project by L&T Realty Limited, developed on a 20-acre land parcel in Sector 86, Gurgaon, with a development potential of approximately 3.6 million sq. ft.

Is L&T Realty a trusted developer?

L&T Realty is the real estate arm of Larsen & Toubro – India's largest engineering and construction conglomerate, with 85+ years of operations and a 70 million sq. ft. real estate portfolio.

What is the price of apartments in L&T Sector 86?

Prices start at ₹5 Cr* onwards. Register your interest on this page to receive the latest price updates.

Is L&T Sector 86 RERA registered?

The project is a new launch. RERA registration has been applied for, and the registration number will be updated on this page as soon as it is available. Buyers are advised to verify registration on the HRERA portal.

What are the configurations available?

The project offers 3 BHK and 4 BHK high-rise residences across 7 towers. Phase 1 launches 3 towers with 450 units: two 3 BHK towers and one 4 BHK tower.

What is the location advantage of Sector 86?

Sector 86 offers easy access to NH-48, Dwarka Expressway and the Southern Peripheral Road. IGI Airport is approximately 30 km away, and key employment hubs in Cyber City and Udyog Vihar are reachable within 35 to 45 minutes.

Is this L&T Realty's first project in Gurgaon?

Yes. This is L&T Realty's first land acquisition in the NCR micro-market, making it the builder's debut luxury residential project in Gurgaon.
